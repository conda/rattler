Trust policy: which signing identity is accepted and how strictly verification failures are treated.

Structs§

Identity
The identity (Subject Alternative Name) of a signing certificate, e.g. a GitHub Actions workflow identity such as https://github.com/org/repo/.github/workflows/build.yml@refs/heads/main.
Issuer
The OIDC issuer that vouched for a signing identity.
Publisher
A trusted publisher: constraints on the identity and issuer of a signing certificate. A constraint that is None accepts any value.
VerificationConfig
Configuration shared by the Warn and Require verification modes.
